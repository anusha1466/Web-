Welcome to the MCA Department

The Master of Computer Applications (MCA) department aims to provide quality education and research in the field of computer science and applications. Our department focuses on developing students' technical skills and preparing them for industry challenges.

About the Department

The department offers a three-year postgraduate program in computer applications with the following objectives:

  • Strong foundation in software development and programming.
  • Exposure to modern technologies like AI, Data Science, and Cloud Computing.
  • Encouragement for research and innovation.
